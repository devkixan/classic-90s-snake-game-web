Overview 01

  • This Privacy Policy explains how Classic 90s Snake Game (the “app”) handles information. The game is designed for offline play: it does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, precise location, or payment information to play.

  • Developer Dev Kixan

  • Last updated Nov 1, 2026

Your data 02

  • Stored On device

    The app saves gameplay preferences, unlocked themes and filters, sound and vibration choices, selected game settings, and high scores locally on your device. This information is used only to remember your choices and game progress. We do not receive this local game information on our servers.

  • Removing it

    You can remove this information by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it. For a browser version, clear the site data in your browser. This may reset your saved preferences, unlocked items, and scores.

Advertising 03

  • Ads Google Mobile Ads

    Supported native mobile versions of the app may show banner ads and optional rewarded ads. Banner ads may appear in the menu or after a game ends. A rewarded ad is shown only after you choose to watch one to unlock an in-game item or continue a game.

  • Offline Still playable

    Ads need an internet connection; the game remains playable offline when ads are unavailable or you choose not to watch a rewarded ad.

  • What Google processes

    To deliver, measure, secure, and, where permitted, personalize ads, Google and its advertising partners may process your IP address, device and operating-system details, app version, advertising identifier, general location inferred from IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic or fraud-prevention information. The information processed and the ads shown can vary by device, region, and your privacy choices.

  • Analytics None

    We do not use Google Analytics or a separate analytics service in the app.

Your choices 04

  • Consent

    Where required by law, the app presents an advertising consent message before requesting ads. When available, use the app’s Privacy choices option in the main menu to review or change your advertising-consent choices.

    You can also manage advertising settings through your device settings or Google account settings where supported.

Retention 05

  • Game data Until cleared

    Local game information remains on your device until you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app. The developer does not provide user accounts or maintain a separate database of player profiles.

  • Ad data

    Advertising-related information is handled by Google under its own policies. Advertising providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live.

Children 06

  • The app does not knowingly ask users to provide personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to the developer, please get in touch using the contact details below.

Your rights 07

  • Ad data

    For advertising-related data, use the privacy controls described above or contact Google.

  • Requests

    You may also send us a request through the contact form below; we will respond as required by applicable law.
