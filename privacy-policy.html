Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Classic 90s Snake Game (the “app”) handles information. The app is developed by Dev Kixan. The game is designed for offline play: it does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, precise location, or payment information to play.

Information stored on your device

The app saves gameplay preferences, unlocked themes and filters, sound and vibration choices, selected game settings, and high scores locally on your device. This information is used only to remember your choices and game progress. We do not receive this local game information on our servers.

You can remove this information by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it. For a browser version, clear the site data in your browser. This may reset your saved preferences, unlocked items, and scores.

Advertising

Supported native mobile versions of the app may show Google Mobile Ads banner ads and optional rewarded ads. Banner ads may appear in the menu or after a game ends. A rewarded ad is shown only after you choose to watch one to unlock an in-game item or continue a game. Ads need an internet connection; the game remains playable offline when ads are unavailable or you choose not to watch a rewarded ad.

To deliver, measure, secure, and, where permitted, personalize ads, Google and its advertising partners may process your IP address, device and operating-system details, app version, advertising identifier, general location inferred from IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic or fraud-prevention information. The information processed and the ads shown can vary by device, region, and your privacy choices. We do not use Google Analytics or a separate analytics service in the app.

Learn more about Google’s data practices in the information Google receives from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

Where required by law, the app presents an advertising consent message before requesting ads. When available, use the app’s Privacy choices option in the main menu to review or change your advertising-consent choices. You can also manage advertising settings through your device settings or Google account settings where supported.

Retention and deletion

Local game information remains on your device until you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app. Advertising-related information is handled by Google under its own policies. The developer does not provide user accounts or maintain a separate database of player profiles. Advertising providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live.

Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly ask users to provide personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to the developer, please get in touch using the contact details below.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, deletion of, correction of, or information about personal data that we control. Because we do not collect or retain local game data on our servers, clearing the app’s data is normally the way to delete it. For advertising-related data, use the privacy controls described above or contact Google. You may also send us a request through the contact form below; we will respond as required by applicable law.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its services change. The latest version will be available at this page, with its revision date.

Contact

For support, privacy questions, or data-related requests, send us a message through the Contact us form.
