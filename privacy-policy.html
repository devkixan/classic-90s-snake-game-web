Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Classic 90s Snake Game (the “app”) handles information. The app is developed by Dev Kixan.

Information stored on your device

The app saves gameplay preferences, unlocked themes and filters, sound and vibration choices, selected game settings, and high scores locally on your device. This information is used only to remember your choices and game progress. The app does not require an account.

Advertising

The app offers optional banner and rewarded advertising through Google Mobile Ads. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising information, and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads, limit fraud, and comply with legal obligations. The information processed and the ads shown can vary by device, region, and your privacy choices.

Learn more about Google’s data practices in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

Where required by law, the app presents an advertising consent message before requesting ads. When available, use the app’s Privacy choices option in the main menu to review or change your advertising-consent choices. You can also manage advertising settings through your device settings where supported.

Retention and deletion

Local game information remains on your device until you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app. Advertising-related information is handled by Google under its own policies. The developer does not provide user accounts or maintain a separate database of player profiles.

Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly ask users to provide personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to the developer, please get in touch using the contact details below.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its services change. The latest version will be available at this page, with its revision date.

Contact

For support, privacy questions, or data-related requests, send us a message through the Contact us form.
